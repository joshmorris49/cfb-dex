How the stats work

  • HP · Program: roster talent (half), the 2026 recruiting class (a quarter) and the 2026 preseason AP poll (a quarter).
  • Attack · Rush O: rush yards per game, rush TDs per game, yards per carry.
  • Defense · Rush D: rush yards, yards per carry and rush TDs allowed.
  • Sp. Atk · Pass O: pass yards per game, pass TDs per game, passer rating.
  • Sp. Def · Pass D: pass yards allowed, opponent passer rating, interceptions.
  • Speed · Big Play: explosive plays gained and allowed (runs of 20+ yards, completions of 25+), takeaways and giveaways, and special teams (returns, coverage, punting, FG%).

Schedule adjustment

Raw season numbers reward teams that played weak opponents. So each unit is credited for who it faced: a rushing offense is judged against the run defenses on its schedule, a pass defense against the passing offenses it saw, explosive plays against the explosive plays those opponents allow, takeaways against how well opponents protect the ball, and returns against the coverage units they met. Every unit is solved together for all 138 teams from every 2026 game played so far. FCS opponents count as a little weaker than the weakest FBS teams. Program, punting and FG% aren't adjusted.

With only six or seven games played, the adjustment is still noisy. Against this week's SP+ top 25 this ranking shares 20 teams (21 before the adjustment), and it will settle as more games are played. Total also includes Program, which SP+ leaves out.

Scale and matching

Each adjusted rating is ranked against all 138 FBS teams. That standing is then placed on the national dex's own spread for that stat, so a median FBS team gets a median Pokémon value and a team two steps above average gets a value only about 2% of Pokémon reach. Each team's six stats are compared with every Pokémon's base stats, and the set of one-to-one pairings with the smallest total gap wins. Fit is how close the pairing is, out of 100. Most teams get their single closest Pokémon; the rest get their second or third choice because a nearby team needed it more.

Notes
